# Introduction

* Solar energy contributes **133 GW out of 254 GW** of total renewable capacity.
* Solar : Total Renewable ≈ **1 : 1.9**, meaning solar accounts for **nearly half of the renewable mix**.
* Solar thermal systems are limited by **intermittent sunlight**, reducing heat availability during night and cloudy conditions.
* **PCM-based thermal storage** captures excess solar heat and releases it later, improving continuous thermal energy availability.

### Why PCM is used more in foreign countries

* Strong focus on **energy efficiency and carbon reduction**.
* **Government support** and **higher investment capacity** make PCM integration commercially feasible.

### Why PCM is not widely integrated in India

* PCM systems increase **design complexity**.
* With proper optimization and field validation, PCM can significantly improve **hot water availability, sustainability, and efficiency**.

# Motivation

* Thermal energy accounts for **48–50% of global final energy consumption**.

* Residential water heating uses **6–8% of household energy**.

* Conventional Solar Water Heating (SWH) systems achieve **45–70% efficiency**, wasting **30–55% of solar energy** due to poor storage.

* PCM integration can:

  * Improve thermal efficiency
  * Extend heat retention time
  * Store ~50% of total heat with <20% PCM volume.

* Global solar thermal capacity: **~544–560 GWth (2024–2025)**

* Global water heater market: **$23.7B (2023) → $32.1B (2029)**

* Most PCM-SWH systems use **fixed/offline strategies**, causing losses under variable irradiance and demand.

# Objectives

### Overall Problem

Most PCM solar water heating systems use **passive or fixed control rules**, leading to delayed heat availability and energy losses.

### Objectives

* Use **real-time sensor data** to select suitable PCM materials.
* Implement **adaptive AI control for PCM charge/discharge**.
* Design a **fully embedded intelligent controller**.
* Build and test a **complete working prototype** integrating PCM storage and hardware.

# Technical Knowledge

## Core Control Framework

Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) enables **real-time adaptive control** of PCM storage.

Challenges addressed:

* Variable solar irradiance
* Stochastic household demand

### Algorithms

* Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO)
* Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (DDPG)
* Model Predictive Control (MPC)

---

## Thermal Modeling and Optimization

### Grey-Box Thermal Model

Combines **physics-based heat transfer equations** with **data-driven corrections**.

### Enthalpy–Porosity Method

Used for modeling phase change behavior.

### Optimization

Taguchi Method + Grey Relational Analysis used for:

* PCM selection
* Collector efficiency optimization
* Storage duration optimization

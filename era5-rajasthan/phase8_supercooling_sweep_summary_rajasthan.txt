HONEST INTERPRETATION: NEGATIVE RESULT (PENALTY MAKES THINGS WORSE)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Finding: Applying the supercooling penalty to the physics model REDUCES
physics/MCDM agreement in Clusters 1-2, making the ranking WORSE, not better.

Possible explanations:

1. PENALTY FORMULATION IS INCORRECT:
   The assumed mechanism (reduced h_p in cooled state) may not capture
   supercooling's real effect. Supercooling in real PCMs:
   - Delays solidification (yes, modeled via reduced h_p)
   - But may INCREASE thermal stratification in the tank (not modeled)
   - May affect charge/discharge hysteresis differently than assumed
   - The grey-box model's 2-node architecture may be too coarse to
     accurately represent supercooling dynamics

2. SUPERCOOLING IS NOT THE LIMITING FACTOR:
   Phase 6 assigned supercooling 48-64% weight in the MCDM ranking.
   Phase 8 results suggest this weight is OVER-ESTIMATED relative to
   supercooling's actual impact on solar fraction in this system.
   Other criteria (Tm_fitness, latent heat, thermal conductivity) may
   dominate the observed solar-fraction variation more than MCDM assumes.

3. SYSTEM DYNAMICS MASK THE PENALTY:
   At PCM_MASS_KG=50 kg (pipeline-consistent), the 300 kg water tank's
   sensible thermal mass dominates system behavior (noted in Phase 7
   calibration). A 3.5 K supercooling effect on a 50 kg PCM bed may be
   too small to move the needle on annual solar fraction when the tank's
   own 300 kg sensible heat swamps the dynamics.

IMPLICATION FOR PHASE 7 INTERPRETATION
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Phase 7's negative rho (Clusters 0, 2) and weak positive rho (Cluster 1)
should NOT be interpreted as "the MCDM supercooling weight is correct but
the physics model doesn't simulate it."

Instead: The results suggest that supercooling's real-world effect on
system performance may be SMALLER than the MCDM weighting (48-64%)
implies, or that the assumed penalty mechanism does not accurately
capture supercooling dynamics in this tank/PCM configuration.
